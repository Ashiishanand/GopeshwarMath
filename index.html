Introduction

A spiritual home in Braj.

Gopeshwar Kailash Math is being developed in Vrindavan under Kailash Ashram Charitable Trust Kashi. Its purpose is to serve devotees with a place for stay, daily spiritual practice, bhojan prasad, seva and a welcoming atmosphere while they visit Braj.

The vision is rooted in the tradition of Kailash Math, Kashi, and carried forward into a new centre for devotees in Vrindavan.

Spiritual guidance

Swami Ashutoshanand
Giri Ji Maharaj

श्री श्री 1008 महामंडलेश्वर · न्याय वेदांत दर्शनाचार्य

Swami Ashutoshanand Giri Ji Maharaj is the present Mahamandaleshwar associated with Kailash Math, Kashi. His life has been shaped by study, sadhana, teaching and service.

His journey of learning took him from Haridwar to Kashi, where he spent 14 years studying Nyaya, Grammar and Vedanta. He later embraced Sanyas in Kashi on Mahashivratri in March 2014 and continues to share spiritual teachings through Ram Katha, Bhagavat and Gita Gyana.

Gopeshwar Kailash Math is part of his vision to create a place in Braj where devotees can stay, practise, receive prasad and experience the spiritual atmosphere of Vrindavan with care and dignity.
